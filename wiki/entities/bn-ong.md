# BN Ong

> Trader, portfolio manager, and developer of the TAT trading systems and automated screener workflows.

## Background

BN Ong (often referred to as Bini) is an experienced technical analyst and portfolio manager specializing in multi-asset trading (forex, indices, commodities, and stocks). They are the creator of the proprietary TAT trading algorithms. BN Ong advocates for combining disciplined technical structures (such as equidistant channels and liquidity sweeps) with modern AI-driven automation (such as Python trading bots, Google Script sheet scrapers, and Telegram alert channels) to build compounding retirement portfolios.

## Key positions / contributions / events

- **Developer of TAT Systems**: Formulated the consolidation breakout and base-detection algorithms (resembling Cup and Handle patterns). Emphasized that weekly scans have a higher success rate (~64%) compared to daily noise. (see [Weekly Review — 30th May 2026](../sources/2026-05-30-weekly-review.md))
- **Creator of the Wash and Rinse (WR) Pattern**: Developed a precise liquidity sweep system to trade institutional stop-hunts across JPY pairs, indices, and gold. (see [3 Trade Thoughts — 1 June 2026](../sources/2026-06-01-3-trade-thoughts.md))
- **AI Automation Proponent**: Leverages Claude and Gemini to write Google Scripts and Python APIs that scrape email signals and automate live executions directly with brokers. (see [Weekly Review — 30th May 2026](../sources/2026-05-30-weekly-review.md))
- **Technical Market Strategist**: Uses "ball theory" to analyze gold volatility and JPY MACD hidden divergences to forecast yen weakness. (see [Weekly Review — 30th May 2026](../sources/2026-05-30-weekly-review.md))
- **Metal Trend and Rotation Analyst**: Conducted technical mapping on gold, silver, and copper, identifying copper's cup and handle breakout, and gold's 62% Fibonacci support boundaries. (see [Weekly Review (Access Time) — 2nd June 2026](../sources/2026-06-02-weekly-review.md))
- **Developer of the TAT V2 Dashboard**: Introduced default EMA filtration (21 and 133 EMAs) to reduce noise in large watchlists while providing configuration options to scan unfiltered for deep-reversal momentum setups. (see [TAW Pro — 3rd June 2026](../sources/2026-06-03-tat-pro.md))
- **Formulated Moving Average Confluences**: Discovered that when the short-term 20 MA and long-term 133 MA flatten and merge together, they form a powerful resistance/support zone indicating strong trend boundaries. (see [AHH Access Time — 3rd June 2026](../sources/2026-06-03-ahh-access-time.md))
- **Crypto Cycle Analyst**: Predicted long-term Bitcoin distribution patterns, using historical statistics of 70% to 85% cycle drawdowns to target macro bottom bounds at the $25,000–$37,000 region. (see [AHH Access Time — 3rd June 2026](../sources/2026-06-03-ahh-access-time.md))
- **Community Alert Organizer**: Initiated a community stock alert model where members submit watch lists to bypass TradingView's high-tier cost boundaries using a Python-based alert script sending reports directly to Telegram. (see [AHH Access Time — 3rd June 2026](../sources/2026-06-03-ahh-access-time.md))
- **Watchlist Service Launch**: Launched a $60/year community watchlist alert service running on VPS Python automation, sending "Ted" (TAT) signals for up to 30 symbols per market directly to Telegram. (see [Weekly Review — 6th June 2026](../sources/2026-06-06-weekly-review.md))
- **Formulated Multi-Layer Structural Analysis**: Formulated a "layer by layer" (metaphorically "Chin Chen Gao" or seven-layer cake) multi-timeframe analysis method. Price corrections move between nested support/resistance layers; if one layer fails, price falls to the next. (see [Weekly Review — 6th June 2026](../sources/2026-06-06-weekly-review.md))
- **U-Shape Starting Point Projection Method**: Defined a Japanese-inspired technique where static support and resistance lines are projected horizontally from the starting points of previous major U-shape swings. (see [Weekly Review — 6th June 2026](../sources/2026-06-06-weekly-review.md))
- **Solana Execution**: Successfully executed a full exit from Solana (accumulating at 220–250, selling at 400+, and exiting completely when a head-and-shoulders reversal formed) prior to its 33% two-day drop. (see [Weekly Review — 6th June 2026](../sources/2026-06-06-weekly-review.md))
- **Trypophobia**: BN Ong experiences trypophobia (fear of systematic circular patterns, such as certain leaf arrays). (see [Weekly Review — 6th June 2026](../sources/2026-06-06-weekly-review.md))
