> Theory session focusing on the use of Monthly Autoboxes to determine long-term trends and identify major support/resistance levels.

## Technical rules emphasized

- **Monthly Autoboxes**: One box equals 4-5 weeks. Used to track macro trends. A breakout above multiple monthly boxes signifies a major shift in resistance turning to support.
- **Basing for Reversals**: Do not blind-buy at support using limit orders. Wait for the price to "base" and form a confirmed higher high and higher low before entering a long trade.
- **Execution Trigger**: For intraday/short-term entries, wait for an H4 close above the opening bar high and the bullish wash line (Magenta line) to confirm a reversal.
