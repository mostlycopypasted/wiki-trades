> Theoretical session on wash and rinse logic, relative strength analysis between China A50 (CN50) and other index charts, and structural corrections in Gold (XAUUSD) and Cocoa (CC1!).

## Key claims

- **[[CN50]] (China A50)**: Bullish. Demonstrates high relative strength compared to other indices by remaining far above its orange resistance level after breaking out. Trade pullbacks near the broken resistance boundary.

Reversal requires a clear H1/H4 basing phase (double bottom/inverted H&S neckline breakout) and a higher high/low sequence.
- **[[CC1]] (Cocoa)**: Bullish. Rebounded from key support at 3699. A second H4 signal retest provided a high-probability long entry with a tight stop loss.

## Technical rules emphasized

- **Relative Strength via TAT Line Distance**: Compare relative strength by looking at how far price remains above a broken horizontal resistance. Charts that hold far above the breakout zone are stronger than those that test it repeatedly.
- **Downtrend Reversal Sequence**: Never assume a downtrend has ended at support until the market prints a basing structure (double bottom/inverted H&S), a higher high breakout, and a subsequent higher low.
- **Deep Retracement Discount Rule**: Favour entering long positions at a 60%–70% Fibonacci discount (specifically 78.6%) from the swing low to high, aligning the discount zone with historical horizontal support boundaries.
- **FOMO Prevention discipline**: Discipline yourself to miss initial breakouts and buy high. Only execute when price returns to key horizontal support zones where the stop loss is narrow and manageable.
