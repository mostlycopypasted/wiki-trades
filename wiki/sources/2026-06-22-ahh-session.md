## Technical Rules Emphasized
- **Timeframe Alignment**: When taking a trade based on a lower timeframe (e.g., shorting on H1 breakout), the Take Profit (TP) target must be based on the nearest support of the higher timeframe (e.g., H4).
- **Sideways Markets**: In a sideways range, the strategy is to short at resistance and cover at support, until a definitive breakout occurs.
- **Strong Trends**: For aggressive uptrends (like NASDAQ), if a deep retracement doesn't happen, use a "fast in, fast out" approach with strict trailing stop losses and avoid greed.
- **Execution**: Do not just backtest in hindsight; you must have the psychological confidence to execute the plan when price reaches the zone in real time.
