# Lesson 4: How does Wash and Rinse pattern look like?

> A technical guide from Online Forex Master (The Alien Wash Course) explaining the structural mechanics of the contrarian Wash and Rinse (WR) buy/sell patterns and how to define and utilize "wash lines" to identify and trade institutional liquidity sweeps.

## Key claims

- **Not a Candlestick Pattern**: The Wash and Rinse (WR) is a multi-candle liquidity-sweep structure, not a single candlestick pattern. It can range from a simple 3-bar sequence to a complex pattern of 10 or more bars. (p. 2)
- **Contrarian Mechanics**: The core thesis of the WR pattern is to think like a contrarian by identifying when the price has temporarily moved lower (for buy) or higher (for sell) to grab liquidity, establishing a highly attractive entry price. (p. 2)
- **Buy Setup Mechanics (Bar 0, 1, 3)**:
  - **Bar 0** acts as a neutral starting or reference bar. (p. 2)
  - **Bar 1** closes below Bar 0's low by a minor, insignificant margin, prompting retail trend-followers to think of a bearish breakout. (p. 2)
  - A horizontal **Magenta Wash Line** is drawn at the low of Bar 0. (p. 2, 4)
  - **Bar 3 (Confirmation Bar)** subsequently closes above the Magenta Wash Line, confirming the WR buy pattern. (p. 2, 5)
- **Sell Setup Mechanics (Bar 0, 1, 3)**:
  - **Bar 0** acts as the neutral reference bar. (p. 4)
  - **Bar 1** closes above Bar 0's high by a minor, insignificant margin. (p. 4)
  - A horizontal **Green Wash Line** is drawn at the high of Bar 0. (p. 4)
  - **Bar 3 (Confirmation Bar)** subsequently closes below the Green Wash Line, confirming the WR sell pattern. (p. 4, 5)
- **Bearish Momentum Confluence**: In a sell setup, if the confirmation bar (Bar 3) trades below the low of the breakaway bar (Bar 1), the bearishness is considered significantly stronger. (p. 4)
- **Multi-Line Trend Extension**: If the price continues to trend strongly upward or downward without reversing, multiple successive wash lines can be drawn as new reference bars form. (p. 5)
- **Intraday Fine-Tuning**: Execution is not restricted to waiting for the confirmation candle (Bar 3) to close; traders can fine-tune entries to trade the intraday movement (such as price spikes) within Bar 3 itself to optimize the risk-to-reward ratio. (p. 4)

## Notable quotes

> WR is not a candlestick pattern. The WR pattern can be as simple as a 3-bar pattern to as many as 10 or more bars pattern. (p. 2)

> However, the concept of WR is to think like a contrarian – only when price has moved lower than it is at an attractive price. (p. 2)

> For a buy WR, we always use a Magenta color wash line. For a sell WR, we use a Green color wash line. (p. 4)

> Hence, there can be many wash line if price continues to move up or down without reversing. (p. 5)
