## 🔍 Multi-Timeframe Context
- H1 timeframe is always the first to show actionable structural changes (minor higher highs / lower lows and wash & rinse triggers) before H4 or Daily confirm.
- Cross-referencing lower timeframe entries (H1/M15) with major Daily support lines (e.g. [[JP225]] daily green level or [[XAUUSD]] H1 wash lines) improves risk/reward ratio.

## 📐 Technical Trading Rules Emphasized
- **Shorting Rule**: Never short at the bottom of a support move; wait for price to rally back up into resistance and generate a clear signal/retest.
- **Singapore Stock Rule**: Avoid using H1 charts on SGX stocks due to low liquidity and half-day trading sessions skewing indicator readings. Use Daily charts only.
- **Yen Cross Preference**: Prefer [[AUDJPY]] for long positions over other yen crosses due to cleaner daily structural patterns. Avoid shorting JPY overall.
- **Profit Management**: In range-bound markets or ahead of major earnings releases, emphasize short-term trades and lock in profits at key structure levels rather than holding long-term.
