## 📐 Technical Trading Rules Emphasized

- **The Retest Mechanics & 2nd Test Rule**:
  - In an uptrend, price retracing back to a previous high forms a "mini-downtrend" (lower highs & lower lows).
  - A single bounce off support is unverified. The safest and highest-probability entry occurs on the **2nd test (retest)** in the same support zone.
- **Reversal Pattern Evolution (Double Bottom vs Inverted Head & Shoulders)**:
  - If a retest overshoots the 1st support test, it morphs from a double bottom into an **Inverted Head & Shoulders**.
  - Rule: The right shoulder retest must **not go lower than the head**, and must break above the neckline resistance to confirm the trend reversal.
- **With-Trend vs. Counter-Trend Retest Priority**:
  - **Scenario 1 (With-Trend)**: Retest at support in an uptrend (or retest at resistance in a downtrend) carries the highest probability of success.
  - **Scenario 2 (Counter-Trend)**: Taking a double bottom long in a macro downtrend is a temporary range trade. **Must take profits at the neckline**.
- **Trade Management & Trailing SL Discipline**:
  - Combine trailing stops with **partial profit-taking** at key resistance levels to manage trading psychology and eliminate fear of loss.
- **Line Chart Filtering**:
  - Switch to a **Line Chart** to filter out candlestick wicks/tails and clearly identify true structural Higher Highs, Lower Lows, and Neckline breakouts.
