# Weekly Review 2026-06-27

> Weekly review session focusing on U.S. Dollar macro strength (DXY), bearish head-and-shoulders continuation in Euro (EURUSD), Bitcoin's structural break below 72.5k, and opportunities in indices (Nikkei 225) and commodities (Crude Oil support).

## Key claims

- **[[DXY]] (US Dollar Index)**: Bullish. Confirmed monthly close of long-term bullish trend. Major resistance at 105; a close above it confirms multi-year trend continuation. Safe-haven status and rate hike expectations continue to support the dollar.
- **[[EURUSD]] (Euro vs US Dollar)**: Bearish. Long-term head & shoulders pattern (originating from 2009) is active, with price establishing lower lows. Retracements on daily/weekly charts represent continuation short setups.
- **[[AUDJPY]] (Aussie Yen)**: Bearish. Broke daily support and retraced, but lacks a clear weekly lower low. Support is active on weekly, but daily bias is down.

## Technical rules emphasized

- **Winter/Summer Seasonality Analogy (Relevance)**: Only analyze/trade timeframes and structures when they are directly relevant (e.g. looking at monthly charts only when price is at multi-year historical boundaries, otherwise focus on daily/weekly).
- **Automated Execution Patience**: Automated systems and AI excel by waiting patiently for precise retracement zones (e.g. buying pullbacks at level 5/6 of a range to minimize stop-loss size relative to the base of the swing).
- **Structure Breakdown Validation**: True structural breakouts (such as [[BTCUSD]]'s 72.5k line or [[HSI]]'s 24.3k neckline) require a clear break, retracement/retest, and a subsequent break of the new low to confirm invalidation.
- **Yen Pair Divergence**: Favour trading the dominant currency driver in Yen crosses rather than assuming general Yen weakness when charts like AUDJPY show support breaks while others remain supported.
