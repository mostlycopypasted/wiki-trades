Session reviewing the application of TAT in different timeframes, the new Telegram alerts system, and market analysis for [[EURUSD]], [[DXY]], [[COPPER]], and the Healthcare Sector.

## Key Claims & Observations

- **TAT Telegram Alerts**: The H1 and H4 TAT alerts are prioritized for active trading instruments (currencies, indices, gold). Stocks are better suited for daily/weekly tracking via Excel due to their slower intra-day characteristics (07:14, 14:46).
- **Timeframe Hierarchy & Reconciliation**: Always rely on higher timeframe trends while trading short-term impulse waves for quality trades (37:10-37:15). If a macro timeframe shows a downtrend but the micro timeframe shows an uptrend, it signifies price retracing higher to offer an optimal entry for a short position.
- **Recency Effect in Market Structure**: When the market exhibits conflicting waves (a large macro wave vs. a smaller recent wave), traders should assume the smaller, more recent wave is in control until it breaks.
- **Confluence Analysis**: Combine TAW signals with traditional historical support/resistance levels for better trade decisions (29:10-29:36).
- **Pattern Recognition**: Only buy bullish when price shows a reversal pattern after coming down; don't buy in the middle of uptrends (43:50-44:02).
- **Support/Resistance Tests**: It's the test of resistance that matters, not just the break; price may hit resistance and reverse (46:57-47:03).
- **Wait for Confirmation**: Before entering bullish trades, wait for TAT signals to confirm trend changes; don't trade prematurely when support holds (39:01-39:48).
- **Risk Management**: Place alerts around key support/resistance levels for nimble trading with quick profit-taking, especially in choppy markets with multiple support levels (46:06, 47:17).
