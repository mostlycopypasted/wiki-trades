## Technical Trading Rules Emphasized

- **Price Sequencing Importance**: Signal timing and price sequencing are critical for trade strength. A bullish signal appearing after a higher high and higher low is significantly stronger than one appearing during a downtrend [50:05]
- **Break of Structure**: Wait for confirmed break of structure on lower timeframes (4-hour, 1-hour) before entering trades. Do not enter prematurely [10:31]
- **Wash and Rinse Lines**: Monitor whether wash and rinse lines act as resistance or support. A level that previously held support may become resistance if broken [06:48]
- **Multiple Timeframe Confirmation**: Align signals across daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour timeframes. Confluence of levels across timeframes increases trade quality [24:03]
- **Retracement Completion**: Only enter long positions after sufficient retracement to support levels or wash and rinse lines. Avoid entering when price is too far extended [23:14]
- **Pattern Confirmation**: Patterns like head and shoulders, double tops, and inverted head and shoulders require neckline breaks and higher low/higher high formations for confirmation [29:01]
- **TAT Guidance**: Trust TAT calculations for support/resistance zones and turning points. TAT color changes (green/blue for bullish, red/orange for bearish) indicate trend direction [49:04]
- **No Guaranteed Retracements**: Retracements are not guaranteed. If price does not retrace to expected levels, be prepared to enter on market order or adjust strategy [37:13]
- **Wait for Clear Signals**: Patience is essential. Do not force trades when signals are unclear or marginal. Wait for unambiguous price action [54:00]
- **Multi-Year Breakouts**: When price breaks out of multi-year consolidation (18+ years), expect significant momentum. These are high-probability setups [43:48]
