## Technical Trading Rules & Techniques Emphasized

- **Rule 1: Multi-Layer Structural Analysis ("Chin Chen Gao" / Seven-Layer Cake)**: Analyze market support/resistance levels as concentric, overlapping layers across multiple timeframes. If a smaller intraday layer (e.g. H4) breaks, price shifts to the next larger layer of support/resistance. Trade the space in between layers and exit/take profit when price meets the next layer boundaries.
- **Rule 2: U-Shape Starting Point Projection**: A Japanese-inspired method of identifying static support and resistance. Instead of arbitrary lines, project key price levels horizontally from the starting points of previous major U-shape structures. The distance and depth of these U-shapes define structural turning points.
- **Rule 3: Retracement Alert and Lower Timeframe Entry**: To capture high-probability entries (e.g. shorting EURUSD), place alerts slightly below the key higher-timeframe (H4) resistance zone. Once alerted, zoom in to the lower timeframe (H1) and execute the trade only when a matching continuation pattern forms.
- **Rule 4: Cycle Drawdown Math for Macro Support**: In high-volatility assets like crypto, ignore short-term noise and use historical cycle drawdown statistics (e.g., BTC's 80% and ETH's 80-92% drawdowns) to establish ultimate support floor buying targets (e.g., 25,000 for BTC).
