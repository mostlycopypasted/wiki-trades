## Technical rules emphasized

- **Gap down/up Significance**: Weekly open gaps (such as in [[DXY]] or [[NASDAQ]]) signal strong institutional directional bias. If the gap remains unfilled, trade in the direction of the gap.
- **Divergence in Related Assets**: When two highly correlated indices (like [[CN50]] and [[HK50]]) differ (CN50 cleared daily wash lines while HK50 remained trapped), focus capital on the cleaner, stronger chart ([[CN50]]).
- **Confluence for Shorting**: Favour short entries at major resistance confluences (e.g. daily red level + weekly wash line on [[BTCUSD]]) rather than chasing a weak trend in no-man's-land.
