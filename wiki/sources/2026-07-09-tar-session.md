## Key Technical Trading Rules Emphasized

- **Consistency Check Across Pairs [07:04]**: Verify if bearish/bullish signals originate from one currency or broad-based momentum by cross-checking related pairs (Euro pairs for Euro weakness, NZD pairs for NZD strength)
- **Filter Lone Signals [15:45]**: Avoid "one-man show" trades where only a single pair triggers with no consistency across related pairs; probability of success is lower unless at very strong technical levels
- **TAT Resistance/Support Levels [20:25]**: Use TAT-identified tag resistance and horizontal resistance lines; multiple tests of resistance increase confidence in trade setups
- **Four-Point Trend Confirmation [40:08]**: For downtrend confirmation, require four points: high → lower high → low → lower low before drawing downward channels and entering shorts with confidence
- **Higher Timeframe Integration [45:51]**: Cross-validate setups using weekly, daily, and H4 charts; identify key zones where multiple timeframes align to increase trade confidence
- **Avoid Shorting at Support [38:05]**: Do not take bearish signals at daily support levels on intraday timeframes; short from resistance zones (top of channels) for better risk-to-reward; shorting at support has very low probability
- **Trade Management at Key Levels [32:59]**: At significant support zones, either take interim profit, final TP, or trail stop loss; be prepared for reversals if support holds
- **Front Runner Validation [15:22]**: If one pair triggers first, confirm it's a front runner by checking if related pairs also trigger in the next H4 candle; otherwise filter it out
- **Pattern Recognition & Probability [42:13]**: Use weather pattern analogy—repeated price behavior at same levels increases probability of similar outcomes; wait for pattern confirmation before entering
