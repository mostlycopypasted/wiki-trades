## Key Technical Trading Rules Emphasized

- **Wait for Confirmation Signals**: Do not enter trades prematurely; wait for price confirmation at key levels before trading
- **Range Trading Approach**: Most markets are currently in consolidation/range-bound phases; focus on trading defined ranges rather than trending moves
- **Support/Resistance Levels**: Never short into support levels or long into resistance levels; wait for retracement back to these zones before entering
- **Retracement-Based Entries**: Preferred entry method is to wait for retracement back into support/resistance levels after initial breakout confirmation
- **Stop-Loss Management**: Place stop-losses carefully at defined technical levels; be aware of wide stop-loss zones and avoid trading too close to take-profit levels
- **Nimble & Quick Trading**: In range-bound markets, execute quick and nimble trades; do not hold positions longer than necessary
- **Higher Timeframe Confirmation**: Use higher timeframes (daily/weekly) to identify key levels, then execute trades on lower timeframes (H1/H4)
- **Pattern Recognition**: Look for technical patterns (cup and handle, triangles, head and shoulders) to identify trade direction and confirm bias
- **Indices Over Individual Stocks**: Prefer trading indices over individual stocks due to clearer technical zones and known resistance levels
- **No Trade = Walk Away**: If there is no clear setup or confirmation signal, do not force a trade; wait for the next opportunity
