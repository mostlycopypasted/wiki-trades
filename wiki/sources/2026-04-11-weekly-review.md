- **TAT Confirmation Required**: Do not trade bullish signals until TAT (higher timeframe support) confirms strength. Avoid premature entries on lower timeframe signals alone [08:28].
- **Support/Resistance Interaction**: Monitor interaction between higher timeframe predicted levels and lower timeframe support/resistance. Slight variations between timeframes are acceptable [21:20].
- **Consolidation Precedes Moves**: Time heals all structures. Consolidation phases are normal before strong directional moves. Length of consolidation matters [17:26], [23:06].
- **Moving Average Confirmation**: Use moving averages as reference points for trend confirmation. Price must stay above moving averages for uptrend validity [26:07].
- **Weekly Close Importance**: Focus on weekly closes for trend confirmation. Multiple price fits must align at key levels to confirm structure completion [10:13].
- **Maintain Watchlists & Alerts**: Keep signal alerts active for semiconductors and tech stocks to identify breakout opportunities [24:25].
- **Currency Pair Selection**: Choose currency pairs wisely for trading vs investing. Yen pairs show trending behavior even when dollar ranges [37:31], [40:04].
- **Range Trading Strategy**: In range-bound markets, trade support and resistance bounces rather than expecting breakouts [49:53].
- **Key Level Breakout Rule**: Confirm trend reversal only after breaking ALL resistance levels in sequence, not just one [47:22].
