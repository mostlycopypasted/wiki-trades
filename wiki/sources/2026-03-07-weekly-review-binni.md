## Technical rules emphasized

- **Month-End Close Significance**: Large structural trend shifts (like ending a multi-year correction in [[USOIL]]) require validation on the monthly close rather than daily/weekly charts.
- **Support Retest Confirmation**: Do not chase breakouts. Enter long positions during shallow or deep retracements into key horizontal zones (like the $156.70 level on [[USDJPY]] or $5001 on [[XAUUSD]]).
- **Safe Haven vs. Volatility**: Geopolitical tensions (e.g. Middle East) drive speculative volatility in [[USOIL]], while macro flows into [[XAUUSD]] and indices like [[HSI]]/[[STI]] behave as clean take-profit rotations.
