## 📐 Technical Trading Rules Emphasized
- **Double-Week Bar Settlement**: When a single weekly bar closes above the tails/lows of two preceding weeks AND above a bullish wash line, it marks an institutional reversal divider.
- **Gap-Down Reversal Protocol**: Following a weekend/session gap down (e.g. DXY, Gold), wait for the gap to fill or for lower timeframe (H1) higher-high confirmation before taking position.
- **Inverted Head & Shoulders Rule**: Never trade pattern anticipation without confirmation; wait for the daily/H4 line chart to breach the neckline and print a higher high.
