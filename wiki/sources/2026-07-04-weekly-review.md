## Trading Rules Emphasized
- **Low Hanging Fruits**: Focus on big, lengthy patterns across a wide universe of instruments. Wait for the same setup repeatedly rather than forcing bad entries.
- **Don't Jump at Lows**: Even if a breakdown is confirmed (e.g. EURGBP), jumping in late exposes you to wide stop losses. Wait for the retracement to a broken support (now resistance).
- **Time to Heal**: Markets require proportional time to consolidate after large drops. Don't mistake a first bounce for a full trend reversal.
