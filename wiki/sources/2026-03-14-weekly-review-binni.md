## Technical rules emphasized

- **Line Chart for Reversal Clarity**: Favour line charts over candlestick charts to identify structural patterns like head-and-shoulders and lower highs/lows (as seen in [[XAUUSD]]'s breakdown).
- **Flexible execution**: Instill retail discipline by trading only clean setups at the bottom (shallow pullbacks) and executing fast-in, fast-out profit takes (e.g. [[ETHUSD]] at $2200).
- **Low-Hanging Fruit Focus**: Do not force trades in highly volatile, messy instruments (like [[USOIL]] inside a massive weekly doji range). Shift capital to cleaner trends (e.g. Dollar strength).
