## Technical rules emphasized

- **Pattern Duration Over Complexity**: Sincerity and simplicity in reading pattern duration beats flowery, complex indicators. Lengthy consolidations flush out weak players and lead to the strongest moves.
- **Support-to-Stop Loss Proximity**: Avoid trades where the entry and stop loss are too far apart (poor risk-management). Wait for retracements/consolidation to bring the stop loss closer.
- **Unfiltered Scanning Stance**: Prefer unfiltered dashboard alerts over heavily filtered ones to scan a wider pool of raw setups, choosing manually which confluences to execute.
