## Technical rules emphasized

- **Line Chart for Pivot Tracking**: Use line charts to trace structural pivot sequences (H-HH-L-HL and L-LL-H-LH) to filter out volatile candlestick tails and define clean resistance boxes.
- **Neckline Break Confirmation**: Bearish structures (like head & shoulders or double tops) are only validated once the neckline support breaks conclusively. Do not execute trend-reversal trades prematurely.
- **Yen Pair Selection Rule**: When Yen pairs show bearish momentum, filter out USDJPY since USD strength will offset Yen strength. Focus instead on pairs like EURJPY, CADJPY, or AUDJPY.
- **Basing for Bottom-Fishing**: Never long a sharp downtrend without first observing a clear basing phase (e.g. double bottom, inverted head-and-shoulders, or a higher high followed by a higher low structure).
