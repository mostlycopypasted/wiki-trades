## 📐 Technical Trading Rules Emphasized

- **The D-R-H-R Formula**:
  1. **D (Day Chart)**: Establish macro support/resistance boundaries & Daily Target Profit.
  2. **R (Retest)**: Require a **2nd test** into support to confirm level firmness & set stop-loss baseline.
  3. **H (Higher High / Lower Low)**: Wait for H1 trendline breakout and structural Higher High.
  4. **R (Retracement)**: **Never buy initial breakouts**; wait for M15 pullback to previous H1 breakout high to execute entry.
- **2nd Test Rule**: A single touch of a support boundary is unverified. A level is only valid when price returns for a 2nd test.
- **Position Sizing**: Use 0.01 micro-lots (on $500 accounts) to avoid margin stress.
- **Trailing Stop Discipline**: Trail stop-loss behind H1 higher lows as price advances to lock in risk-free profit.
