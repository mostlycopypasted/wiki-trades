## Technical rules emphasized

- **Gap Fills as Boxes**: Gaps act as temporary consolidation boxes. Price must conclusively break out of the gap-fill box to establish directional momentum.
- **Structural Break for Reversals**: To short a counter-trend move (e.g., [EURJPY](../entities/eurjpy.md)), do not front-run. Wait for the lower timeframe (H1) to break its support series and form a lower low, then short the lower high retracement.
- **Trailing Stops in Blue Sky**: When an asset (like [JPN225](../entities/jpn225.md)) is at all-time highs with no overhead resistance, aggressively manage risk using trailing stop losses rather than hard Take Profits.
