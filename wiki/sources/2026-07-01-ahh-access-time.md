# AHH Access Time (2026-07-01)

Session led by Pauline covering the start of the new month, DXY's weekly levels, Euro weakness, and navigating Non-Farm Payroll (NFP) volatility.

## Key Claims & Observations

- **[[DXY]]**: Formed a weekly bearish wash and rinse setup, but the trend remains strong up. Testing weekly green levels. If support breaks, expecting 101.8. 
- **[[EURUSD]]**: Euro is the weakest of the majors. Achieved a clear break of structure on H1/H4 (lower lows). Any retracement to resistance is a short opportunity.
- **[[EURGBP]]**: Pointed out a large multi-week head and shoulders breakdown.
- **[[EURNZD]] (Euro Kiwi)**: At a weekly resistance level, channel top.
- **[NZDCHF](../entities/nzdchf.md) (Kiwi Frank)**: Waiting for retracement to finish. No clear break of structure yet on H1/H4 (still making higher highs/lows).
- **[FRA40](../entities/fra40.md) (France 40)**: Monthly uptrend remains intact despite choppiness. H4 shows tons of support.
- **[[SPX]] (S&P 500)**: Highlighted the possibility of sideways ranging (like in March/April) rather than an immediate directional break.
- **[[XAUUSD]] (Gold)**: Monthly close was bearish, breaking 3M and 6M TAT levels. Resting near the quarterly wash and rinse line. Approaching deep correction zones.
- **[[CN50]]**: Monthly bearish wash and rinse line formed. H1 shows clear break of structure to the downside.

## Trading Rules Emphasized
- **Wait for Break of Structure (BOS)**: Before entering a trend continuation, ensure the lower timeframe (H1) has clearly broken structure (e.g. forming a lower low) to confirm the retracement is over.
- **Fibonacci Retracements**: The 'Golden Pocket' (62% to 78%) is the ideal retracement zone for trend entries after a breakout.
- **News Volatility (NFP)**: For high-impact news like NFP or BOJ intervention, if you fear a rippling effect across all pairs, the best trade is often no trade (stay out of the market).

## Connections
- Validates the [Wash and Rinse](../concepts/wash-and-rinse.md) methodology on Monthly and Quarterly timeframes (especially noted on Gold and CN50).
