# Fewer Signals, Higher Hit Rate — My Daily Reversal Method

A daily timeframe trend reversal strategy using specific moving averages combined with the Wash & Rinse pattern, offering fewer but higher-quality setups.

## Key Claims & Methodology
- **Timeframe**: Daily chart for higher reliability.
- **Moving Average Setup**:
  - Mid-term trend: 20, 34, 55 EMAs.
  - Long-term trend: 133, 155 EMAs.
- **Sell Setup Structure**:
  - Wait for price to cross and close completely below both sets of MAs.
  - Wait for a bullish retracement back upwards towards the MAs (creating "wash lines" as bars close above previous highs).
- **Entry Trigger (The Rinse)**:
  - Enter short when a daily bar (the "rinse bar") closes below the nearest wash line.
  - This traps recent buyers, forcing them to exit.
- **Risk & Target**:
  - Stop-loss placed above the high of the wash bar.
  - Minimum profit target is the recent swing low before the retracement.
