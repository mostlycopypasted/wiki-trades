## 📐 Technical Trading Rules Emphasized
- **Trade Execution vs. Limit Orders**: When H1 structure breaks support (e.g., [[HSI]]), limit orders should reference the red breakout level rather than chasing market orders into immediate lower-timeframe support.
- **Partial Take-Profit & Trailing Discipline**: At TP1 take 50% off, at TP2 take an additional 25% (total 75% out), and use a trailing stop on the remaining 25% to maximize runups while protecting gains.
- **Timeframe Selection & Psychology**: Higher timeframes (Weekly/Daily) suit traders prone to noise-induced anxiety. Lower timeframes (H1/M15) require strict limit order rules and tolerance for rapid fluctuations.
