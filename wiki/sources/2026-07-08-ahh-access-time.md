# AHH Access Time (2026-07-08)

Mid-week update on dollar trends, CADJPY structure breaks, and index exhaustion.

## Key Claims & Observations

- **[[DXY]] (Dollar Index)**: Still well supported at the horizontal zone. If support holds, it might resume its uptrend and head toward TP 101.28.
- **[[CADJPY]]**: The H4 candle closed above both the daily and weekly bullish wash lines. Waiting for a retracement to form a higher low to enter long.
- **[[EURCAD]]**: A break of structure occurred on the 1-hour chart, signaling potential short-term bearishness, but waiting for the 4-hour structure to break as well.
- **[[JPN225]]**: The gap was successfully filled, but no bullish signal materialized. Instead, the trend turned short-term bearish, slicing through support.
- **[[SPX]] (S&P 500)**: Exhausted a long bar into resistance (around 7515). Held off on taking a short trade because the range was already exhausted; waiting for a retracement to support instead. 
- **[[CN50]]**: Trading at a long-term trendline support (dating back to Sept 2024). Looking for a bounce, but the nearest bullish wash line is far away, requiring a wide stop loss.
- **[[USOIL]] (Crude Oil)**: Needs to base and form a higher low to change trend. H4 resistance levels are clustered closely together, meaning a breach could rapidly shift momentum.
- **[[GOOGL]] (Google)**: Following last week's bullish wash setup, the down-trend is not definitively over. Needs more confirmation (higher high, higher low) before buying into the reversal.

## Trading Rules Emphasized
- **Risk Management at Exhaustion**: Avoid entering trades (e.g., shorting S&P 500) right after a massive "long bar" exhaustion move, even if price hits a key level. Wait for retracement to manage the risk-to-reward ratio.
- **Waiting for Structural Confirmation**: As seen with Google, a single bullish wash and rinse cross is not always enough to reverse a down-trend; secondary confirmation (like a higher high/higher low sequence) is required.
