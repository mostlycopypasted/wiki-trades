# Trading Confluence

## Definition

Trading Confluence is the technical analysis practice of identifying areas where multiple independent trading indicators, support/resistance levels, chart patterns, and timeframes intersect at a single price level, providing higher probability trading execution setups.

## Origin

Derived from standard technical analysis concepts, and formalized under the TAT and Wash and Rinse trading systems where trades are only executed when static horizontal levels align with dynamic momentum indicators (EMAs) and multi-timeframe boundaries.

## Key claims

- **Line Chart for Reversal Clarity**: Favour line charts over candlestick charts to filter out noise, exposing clear structural setups, head-and-shoulders necklines, and lower highs/lows (as seen in [[XAUUSD]]'s macro correction). (see [Weekly Review Binni 2026-03-14](../sources/2026-03-14-weekly-review-binni.md); [Weekly Review 2026-06-13](../sources/2026-06-13-weekly-review.md))
- **Support-to-Stop Loss Proximity**: High probability setups must have tight proximity between entry levels and stop-loss levels. Lengthy consolidation patterns are favoured because they compress volatility and draw the stop-loss closer while flushing out weak players. (see [Weekly Review Binni 2026-03-14](../sources/2026-03-14-weekly-review-binni.md); [Weekly Review 2026-06-13](../sources/2026-06-13-weekly-review.md))
- **Confluence for Shorting**: Trade short setups only at major resistance confluences (e.g. daily red level + weekly wash line on [[BTCUSD]]) rather than chasing a weak trend in no-man's-land. (see [AHH Access Time 2026-06-15](../sources/2026-06-15-ahh-access-time.md))
- **Pattern Duration Over Complexity**: Sincerity and simplicity in reading pattern duration beats flowery, complex indicators. Lengthier patterns represent major structural zones that strong players defend. (see [Weekly Review 2026-06-13](../sources/2026-06-13-weekly-review.md))
