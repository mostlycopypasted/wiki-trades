# Market Structure

## Definition

Market Structure is the study of trend direction based on price action pivot points. An **Uptrend** is defined by a sequence of **High (H), Higher High (HH), Low (L), and Higher Low (HL)**. A **Downtrend** is defined by a sequence of **Low (L), Lower Low (LL), High (H), and Lower High (LH)**.

## Origin

Originating from Dow Theory, this is the foundational methodology for verifying trend direction and identifying reversals across all asset classes under the TAT and Wash and Rinse systems.

## Key claims

- **Uptrend Validation (H-HH-L-HL)**: An uptrend remains structurally intact as long as new swings form higher highs and higher lows. Retracements into the higher low zone represent optimal buying opportunities. (see [Weekly Review Binni 2026-03-07](../sources/2026-03-07-weekly-review-binni.md); [Weekly Review Binni 2026-03-14](../sources/2026-03-14-weekly-review-binni.md))
- **Downtrend Validation (L-LL-H-LH)**: A downtrend remains structurally intact as long as swings form lower lows and lower highs. Retracements into the lower high zone represent optimal shorting opportunities. (see [Weekly Review Binni 2026-03-14](../sources/2026-03-14-weekly-review-binni.md); [Weekly Review 2026-06-13](../sources/2026-06-13-weekly-review.md))
- **Structure Breakdown Alert**: If the price closes past the latest Higher Low in an uptrend, or the latest Lower High in a downtrend, the trend structure is invalidated, prompting stop-loss execution or reversing the bias. (see [Weekly Review Binni 2026-03-14](../sources/2026-03-14-weekly-review-binni.md); [AHH Access Time 2026-06-15](../sources/2026-06-15-ahh-access-time.md))
