# Forex Trading

## Definition

Forex Trading (Foreign Exchange Trading) is the decentralized global market for the buying, selling, exchanging, and speculating of currencies, where market participants utilize technical and fundamental analysis to capitalize on currency pair price fluctuations.

## Origin

The modern forex market emerged in the early 1970s following the collapse of the Bretton Woods system, which transitioned global finance from fixed exchange rates pegged to the gold standard to a floating rate system governed by supply and demand.

## Key claims

- **Monthly Close Analysis**: Utilizing monthly candle closes provides highly stable macro-trend confirmation. Breaks above or below historical monthly highs/lows (e.g., closing above the previous 4 months of highs) signal long-term structural momentum changes. (see [Trade Thoughts 1 June 2026](../sources/2026-06-01-3-trade-thoughts.md))
- **Wash and Rinse Entries**: Identifying key support/resistance lines where liquidity resides ("wash lines") allows traders to execute highly efficient entries on pullbacks rather than chasing breakouts. (see [Trade Thoughts 1 June 2026](../sources/2026-06-01-3-trade-thoughts.md))
- **MACD Hidden Continuation Divergence**: Sideways currency consolidations (specifically JPY pairs) where price is flat but MACD is correcting in the opposite direction indicate a strong hidden continuation divergence, forecasting macro trend resumption (e.g., yen weakening). (see [Weekly Review — 30th May 2026](../sources/2026-05-30-weekly-review.md))
- **Flat Market Extreme-Only Execution**: During prolonged sideways consolidation in indices or DXY, setups must only be taken at the absolute support/resistance extremes, while avoiding trading in the middle of ranges due to low liquidity and directionless churn. (see [Weekly Review — 30th May 2026](../sources/2026-05-30-weekly-review.md))
- **Lower Timeframe Neckline Executions**: In low-volatility or consolidating forex markets (such as AUDUSD), trading right-shoulder pullbacks within head-and-shoulders structures provides optimal reward-to-risk compared to waiting for a formal breakout. (see [Weekly Review (Access Time) — 2nd June 2026](../sources/2026-06-02-weekly-review.md))
- **Multi-Layered Analysis ("Chin Chen Gao" or Seven-Layer Cake)**: Forex pairs trend and correct through nested support and resistance layers. When a lower timeframe support layer breaks (e.g., NZDJPY 93.86), price falls to the next larger layer of support. (see [Weekly Review — 6th June 2026](../sources/2026-06-06-weekly-review.md))
- **U-Shape Starting Point Projections**: A Japanese-inspired method of drawing static horizontal support and resistance lines projected from the starting points of previous major U-shape structures. (see [Weekly Review — 6th June 2026](../sources/2026-06-06-weekly-review.md))
