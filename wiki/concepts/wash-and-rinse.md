# Wash and Rinse

## Definition

A Wash and Rinse (also referred to as a "stop hunt," "liquidity sweep," or "spring") is a technical analysis pattern where price temporarily breaks through a significant support or resistance level to capture outstanding stop-loss orders (sweeping liquidity) before forcefully reversing in the opposite direction. It is structurally defined as a multi-bar contrarian sequence (often spanning 3 to 10+ bars) requiring a neutral reference bar, a minor breakout bar, and a confirmation reversal bar.

## Origin

Formulated in market-profile and volume-spread analysis (notably by Richard Wyckoff as a "Spring" or "Upthrust"). It describes the behavior of market makers who drive price into dense order areas to fill their own large positions. It has been modernized in retail trading systems like "The Alien Wash Course" by TerraSeeds/Online Forex Master.

## Key claims

- **Wash Line Identification**: The "wash line" represents a major key price level where stop-loss orders are clustered. It is drawn horizontally at the low of a neutral starting bar (Bar 0) for a buy setup, or the high of Bar 0 for a sell setup, once a subsequent breakaway bar (Bar 1) closes past that level. (see [Trade Thoughts 1 June 2026](../sources/2026-06-01-3-trade-thoughts.md); [Lesson 4: Wash and Rinse](../sources/online-forex-master-wash-rinse-pattern.md))
- **Anatomy of the 3-Bar Setup**:
  - **Bar 0 (Neutral Bar)**: Serves as the starting neutral reference point for support or resistance.
  - **Bar 1 (Breakout Bar / Wash Bar)**: Closes below Bar 0's low (buy setup) or above Bar 0's high (sell setup). The breakout margin is sometimes relatively minor ("insignificant") to signify a sweep. However, it can also appear as a **full-bodied solid bar** designed to induce fear and simulate strong momentum, intentionally triggering retail stops. (see [Stop Hunt, Fake Prices](../sources/stop-hunt-fake-prices.md))
  - **Bar 3 (Confirmation Bar / Rinse Bar)**: A subsequent bar (which can occur immediately or up to 10+ bars later) that closes back across the wash line, officially confirming the reversal. (see [Lesson 4: Wash and Rinse](../sources/online-forex-master-wash-rinse-pattern.md))
- **Wash Line as a Boundary**: The wash line functions as a rigid divider between bullish and bearish zones. For example, in a buy setup, as long as price remains above the wash line after the rinse bar closes, the structure is bullish. (see [Stop Hunt, Fake Prices](../sources/stop-hunt-fake-prices.md))
- **High-Probability Locations**: Wash and rinse patterns are most reliable when they occur at significant market structures—such as previous historical highs/lows, major support/resistance zones, or near key moving averages when trading with the overarching trend. (see [Stop Hunt, Fake Prices](../sources/stop-hunt-fake-prices.md))
- **Color Standards**: To ensure high charting clarity, a **Magenta** wash line is always used to mark a buy setup (at Bar 0's low), and a **Green** wash line is always used to mark a sell setup (at Bar 0's high). (see [Lesson 4: Wash and Rinse](../sources/online-forex-master-wash-rinse-pattern.md))
- **Pullback and Intraday Entry Verification**: Once a wash and rinse level is established, entries can be executed at the close of the confirmation bar (Bar 3) or fine-tuned by trading the intraday price spikes/pullbacks directly back to the wash line, optimizing the risk-to-reward ratio. (see [Trade Thoughts 1 June 2026](../sources/2026-06-01-3-trade-thoughts.md); [Lesson 4: Wash and Rinse](../sources/online-forex-master-wash-rinse-pattern.md))
- **Bearish Confluence Rule**: For a sell setup, if the confirmation bar (Bar 3) also trades below the low of the breakaway bar (Bar 1), the bearishness of the reversal is considered significantly stronger. (see [Lesson 4: Wash and Rinse](../sources/online-forex-master-wash-rinse-pattern.md))
- **Multi-Line Trend Extension**: If the price continues to trend strongly in one direction without reversing, multiple successive wash lines are drawn dynamically at each new consecutive low/high as new reference levels are established. (see [Lesson 4: Wash and Rinse](../sources/online-forex-master-wash-rinse-pattern.md))
- **Dampening Volatility (Ball Theory)**: High-volatility wash setups (such as on `[XAUUSD](../entities/xauusd.md)`) require a series of dampening swings (acting like a bouncing ball losing energy) to flush out weak retail longs/shorts before the macro reversal can sustain itself. (see [Weekly Review — 30th May 2026](../sources/2026-05-30-weekly-review.md))
- **Inverted Head-and-Shoulders Confluence**: Lower timeframe setups (such as H1/H4 gold charts) often form inverted head-and-shoulders patterns around the wash line, where the sweep forms the head, and subsequent retests form the shoulders, confirming the support boundary. (see [Weekly Review — 30th May 2026](../sources/2026-05-30-weekly-review.md))
- **Neckline/Shoulder Retest Confirmations**: In complex wash setups on lower timeframes (such as AUDUSD), once a H4 bullish signal breakout occurs, retests of the right shoulder/neckline boundary provide high-reward-to-risk entry zones with tight stop loss bounds below the neckline. (see [Weekly Review (Access Time) — 2nd June 2026](../sources/2026-06-02-weekly-review.md))
- **TAT and EMA Reversal Confluences**: Reversal confluences (often forming around the wash line as inverted head-and-shoulders on gold or silver charts) are validated (the "1+1" rule) when price closes back across the wash line and simultaneously crosses above the short-term 20/21 EMA, signaling that the downward correction has flattened out and momentum is reversing. (see [TAW Pro — 3rd June 2026](../sources/2026-06-03-tat-pro.md))
- **Weekly Rinse Validation (4-Week Low Close)**: A weekly wash/rinse bar (where price closes above a previous high and then closes below it) is highly valid if the close also breaks below a multi-week low (specifically a 4-week low), serving as a powerful bearish continuation signal (e.g. EURUSD). (see [AHH Access Time — 3rd June 2026](../sources/2026-06-03-ahh-access-time.md))
- **Multi-Layer Support/Resistance Transitions**: If a support/wash line on a lower timeframe/smaller layer is broken (e.g., NZDJPY 93.86 support), the trend structure is invalidated, and the price shifts to the next larger layer of support. These transition zones are mapped dynamically from U-shape starting points. (see [Weekly Review — 6th June 2026](../sources/2026-06-06-weekly-review.md))
- **Gap Significance**: Weekly open gaps (such as in [[DXY]] or [[NASDAQ]]) signal strong institutional directional bias. If the gap remains unfilled, it reinforces the direction, setting up a weekly wash and rinse structure in that direction. (see [AHH Access Time 2026-06-15](../sources/2026-06-15-ahh-access-time.md))


## Daily Reversal Method
A specific application of Wash & Rinse for daily timeframe trend reversals, designed to offer fewer but higher-probability setups:
1. **Trend Definition (Moving Averages)**:
   - Mid-term trend is defined by the **20, 34, and 55 EMAs**.
   - Long-term trend is defined by the **133 and 155 EMAs**.
2. **Setup Condition**: For a short setup, wait until price crosses and decisively closes below *both* sets of moving averages (mid-term and long-term). This establishes the overall bearish environment.
3. **The Fake Move (Retracement)**: Wait for price to attempt a retracement back up towards the moving averages. This upward movement is often a "fake move" designed to flush out stops and secure a higher entry price for institutional shorts.
4. **Drawing Wash Lines**: As price retraces upwards, draw a wash line at the low of any bullish bar that closes above the high of the previous bar. Continue drawing new wash lines as price climbs.
5. **Trigger (The Rinse)**: The sell signal triggers when a daily bar (the "rinse bar") closes below the *nearest* (most recent) wash line. This traps the recent buyers, forcing them to exit and fueling the downside reversal.
6. **Risk and Trade Management**:
   - **Stop Loss**: Place the stop loss above the absolute high of the wash/rinse pattern.
   - **First Target**: The minimum profit target is the recent swing low (the low formed before the retracement began).
   - **Trailing**: Once price touches the previous low, take partial profit and trail the stop loss downwards to protect the remaining position (see [Fewer Signals, Higher Hit Rate](../sources/2026-07-08-fewer-signals.md)).
